About Rockline Supplies

Building relationships and supplying the trade since 1987.

Our mission

Rockline Supplies exists to make the supply chain simpler for construction professionals. Whether you're a sole trader building an extension or a main contractor on a commercial development, we aim to be the easiest call you make on a project.

We hold deep stock across eight product categories, run our own delivery fleet, and keep our branch teams properly trained in what they sell. You get the breadth of a national distributor combined with people who actually know their materials.

  • Wholesale pricing with no minimum order threshold
  • 30-day trade account terms for qualified businesses
  • Same-day dispatch on in-stock items before 12:00
  • Technical datasheets for every SKU on request

Our History

From a single yard in Hartfield to a regional network of six branches.

1987

Founded in Hartfield

Robert Lindqvist opens Rockline Supplies from a small timber and aggregate yard on the edge of Hartfield. Initial focus is on local builders and landscapers.

1993

First expansion — Draystone branch

Growing demand from customers in the Draystone corridor leads to the company's first branch outside Hartfield. The Draystone branch opens with 12 staff.

1999

Steel and concrete range introduced

Rockline extends beyond timber and aggregates to offer structural steel sections and bagged concrete products, responding to demand from commercial contractors.

2004

Crestwick and Norbeck branches open

With revenues exceeding £18 million, two further branches open simultaneously. The Hartfield yard is redeveloped as the company's primary distribution hub.

2011

Fleet expansion and same-day delivery launch

Rockline invests in a dedicated delivery fleet, enabling same-day despatch to trade customers within 40 miles of each branch.

2016

Lyndhurst and Pembridge branches open

The sixth and final branch in the current estate opens at Pembridge. Combined headcount reaches 340.

2022

Digital transformation

Introduction of the Rockline trade portal, allowing account customers to place orders, view invoices, and track deliveries online.
